Battery 5v supply

This board lets you use hobby 2-6s Lipo/Li-ion batteries to power random 5v appliances, like charging stuff or powering other projects. It has a 5v output screw terminal up to 3A, and a USB-A port that supports 10W of charging. It also has an LED interface which tells you how charged your battery is.
